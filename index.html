How your data is handled

Nothing you enter is transmitted

These calculators have no server behind them. Every figure is computed in your browser from what you type, and none of them makes a network request of any kind with your data. There is no account, no login, and no submit step.

What is stored, and where

This index page stores nothing. Each calculator saves your inputs in that browser's local storage on the device you are using, so your work survives a reload. It stays on the device until you clear it, and it is not synced or backed up anywhere. Every calculator names its own storage keys in its own copy of this notice and offers a button to delete them.

Share links are the one exception

Where a calculator offers a share link, that link contains your inputs encoded into the URL itself. The data travels wherever you send the link, and it is encoded rather than encrypted, so anyone holding the link can read it. Anything that handles a URL may also retain it, including chat and email services, browser history, and server logs. Treat a share link as you would a document containing the figures in it, because that is what it is.

No tracking of any kind

There is no analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts, and no externally loaded fonts. These pages use only fonts already on your system.

What the host can see

The site is static files served by GitHub Pages. Whoever serves them can see that a browser requested a page, along with the usual things any web server records such as an IP address and a timestamp. They cannot see what you typed, because it never reaches them.
